What it's about

Naruto follows a young ninja from the Hidden Leaf Village who grows up shunned by his neighbors for a secret he didn't choose — a powerful fox spirit sealed inside him at birth. What starts as a story about a loud, overlooked kid chasing a dream to become Hokage turns into a much bigger one about rivalry, loss, and what people owe each other, stretched across two long-running series and hundreds of episodes.

The story, arc by arc

The major stretches of the series, in order.

01

Academy Days

Naruto, Sasuke, and Sakura are put on the same team and learn what it actually means to work together.

02

Chunin Exams

A multi-stage tournament turns into an invasion, and the village's balance of power breaks.

03

Sasuke Retrieval

Sasuke leaves the village chasing power, and Naruto's promise to bring him back sets the tone for everything after.

04

Shippuden Return

Two and a half years later, Naruto comes back stronger, and the shinobi world gets a lot more dangerous.

05

Akatsuki Pursuit

A shadowy organization starts hunting down tailed beasts, and Naruto learns he's on their list.

06

Fourth Shinobi War

Every nation is pulled into one last conflict that finally answers what the series was building toward.

The world

Five nations, hundreds of hidden villages, one very unlucky planet.

The story takes place in a world run by hidden villages of ninja, each loyal to one of five major nations that spend as much time competing with each other as protecting the people they serve. Naruto's home, the Hidden Leaf Village, sits at the center of most of it — not because it's the strongest, but because it's where the story chooses to start.
